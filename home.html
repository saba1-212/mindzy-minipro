What is Mindzy?

Mindzy is an AI-powered study companion that helps students plan smarter, learn faster and stay productive. It combines AI tools, study planning, analytics, notes, quizzes, flashcards and collaborative study rooms into one platform.

📅 Smart Planner

Automatically creates personalized study schedules based on subjects, deadlines and study goals.

🤖 AI Study Engine

Upload PDFs or YouTube lectures and instantly generate summaries, quizzes, flashcards and revision notes.

🔍 Resource Finder

Instantly discover high-quality study materials, notes, tutorials, and learning resources from trusted sources. Search by subject or topic and access the best content to enhance your learning experience.
